🎭 About this set

Twilight Masquerade came out on 24 May 2024. It takes place in Kitakami, the mountain village from the Teal Mask adventure — a land of summer festivals, lanterns and masks.

The star is Ogerpon, who appears with all four of her masks: Teal (24, 25), Hearthflame (40), Wellspring (64) and Cornerstone (112). The village "heroes", the Loyal Three — Okidogi (111), Munkidori (95) and Fezandipiti (96) — are secretly the villains of the story. Other treasures: Bloodmoon Ursaluna (141), six colourful ACE SPEC cards, and the set's most-wanted card, the special illustration Greninja ex (214).

The set has 167 numbered cards plus secret rares up to 226 — with every reverse holo, 373 cards for a master set. In Japan these cards came from two different sets, Mask of Change and Crimson Haze, which is why Japanese cards have other numbers.
